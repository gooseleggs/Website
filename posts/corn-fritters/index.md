These fritters are delish.  Eat with sweet-chili sauce.

**Ingredients**

- 1 egg (egg replacer)
- 3 TBSP water
- 1 tin corn (creamed) – otherwise blend with stick mixer
- 1 onion (small)
- 1/4tsp salt
- Pepper
- 1c Gluten Free Flour
- 1tsp Baking Soda

**Method**

Mix egg replacer.  Finely chop onion.  When egg replacer ready, mix all ingredients into a bowl.

Place dessertspoon size blobs into a frypan (use olive oil to prevent sticking).  Fritters will rise during cooking.  Flip over after a few minutes, or when appears to be half cook and continue to cook on the other side.  Place onto plate to cook when cooked.
